I'm a product leader who builds. I use AI tools to turn ideas into working software, then share what I learn so other product managers can do the same.

Most of my work here is hands on: real apps, real code, and the honest lessons from a PM learning to ship with Claude Code, Cursor, and local models.

**Building better products by building better people.**
